## Getting listings onto Vinted (checked Sept 2026)
- **Nifty can't crosslist to Vinted.** Its docs say Vinted isn't supported.
  Nifty covers Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, Depop, Etsy and Whatnot only.
- Tools that do support Vinted: Vendoo (has a Vinted US page and auto-delist
  for Vinted), List Perfectly (Vinted included on every plan), Crosslist
  (US/UK/CA/AU users, from $29.99/mo).
- Vinted bans or freezes accounts it thinks are automated (big waves in
  Feb and July 2026). Publish by hand, a few at a time, and never use
  auto-relist or bot tools.
- A Vinted sale won't show up in Nifty, so Nifty won't delist it anywhere
  else. Mark it sold in Nifty right away. When something sells elsewhere,
  delete it on Vinted by hand, or use a second tool's auto-delist.
