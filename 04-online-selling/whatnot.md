# Whatnot Show Playbook

How to run the two $1-start shows, **Premium Activewear** and **Premium
Contemporary**, step by step. Covers the item count, how long to talk, the
exact words to say and what to repeat.

Researched September 2026. Whatnot changes its features every month, so
re-check the Help Center links at the bottom every few months.

## How much to trust each line

Every recommendation has a tag:

- **[Whatnot]**: Whatnot's own rules, Help Center, or Whatnot's published data. Proven.
- **[Study]**: a peer-reviewed study. Proven, but not run on Whatnot.
- **[Math]**: my calculation from Whatnot's numbers. The inputs are proven; the result is an estimate.
- **[Test]**: nobody publishes data on this. It's a starting point. Keep it or change it based on your own show numbers.

Whatnot does **not** publish how many items to bring or how long to talk
per item. Those numbers below are **[Math]** or **[Test]**, not facts.

## The numbers at a glance

| What | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Show length | **60–90 min**, never under 15 | Whatnot: avoid ultra-short shows and run "45 to 120+ minute streams". Promoted shows of 10–15 min underperform. **[Whatnot]** |
| Shows per week | **3**, alternating Activewear / Contemporary. Go to 4 once shipping keeps up | Sellers live 3–4×/week average over $13,000/month. Each new show sends followers a fresh notification. **[Whatnot]** |
| Items per 60-min show | **Prep 60, expect to run 45–60** | About 1 item a minute once the opening, repeats and heroes are counted. **[Math]** |
| Best pieces ("heroes") | **6 per 60-min show**, about one every 8–10 min | **[Test]** |
| Buy It Now pieces | **At least 3** in every show | Sellers with 3+ Buy It Now listings in a show earn about 2× more per hour, and about 40% of their sales come from them. **[Whatnot]** |
| Detailed listings | **Every piece.** Brand, size, condition and photo, listed days ahead | 91% of new buyers made their first purchase on a detailed listing, not an "item as shown" one. **[Whatnot]** |
| Auction timer | **10 sec** for regular pieces, **15 sec** for heroes. Try 7 sec once bids come fast | Womenswear's average timer is under 10 sec. 5- and 10-second timers make the most sales per hour. **[Whatnot]** The 15 sec for heroes is **[Test]**. |
| Talk time per regular item | **About 20 seconds** before you start the timer | The required details come to about 50 words, which is roughly 20 seconds at a normal speaking pace. **[Math]** |
| Talk time per hero | **45–60 seconds**, including a try-on | Showing the item on camera lowers buyers' uncertainty and raises purchase intent. **[Study]** The length is **[Test]**. |
| Giveaways | 2 per show (for example at 20 min and 45 min) | Buyers must stay in the show to win, so a giveaway keeps viewers watching. **[Whatnot]** The count is **[Test]**. |

**How the item count was worked out [Math]:** a 60-min show has about 50
minutes of selling once you take out the opening, the repeat lines and the
close. Each regular piece takes about 45 seconds: ~20 s talking, ~10 s timer,
~5–10 s of bid extensions, then the winner shout-out and the next piece. Six
heroes at about 90 s each use 9 minutes. That leaves about 41 minutes for
around 55 regular pieces, so about **60 total**. Your first few shows will be
slower. Leftovers go to the next show or to Buy It Now.

**Starting bids [Whatnot]:** Whatnot's own guidance is that the starting bid
"should reflect the lowest amount you're comfortable accepting." A $1 start
can sell at $1. Any piece you can't let go for $1 should be a Buy It Now or
start higher. Your private pricing rules decide which.

## One week before the show

1. **Schedule the show about a week ahead.** **[Whatnot, Go Live Guide]**
   - Pick the **most specific category** (for example the activewear or swimwear subcategory, not just "Women's Fashion"). Shows only appear in the category you pick. **[Whatnot]**
   - Add **one primary brand tag**: Lululemon for Activewear, Free People for Contemporary. Whatnot tags the other brands from your listings automatically. Shows with a brand tag have higher average sales per show. The tag must match what you're actually selling. **[Whatnot]**
   - **Title:** the hook plus brand names buyers search for, e.g. `$1 PREMIUM ACTIVEWEAR · Lululemon, Free People Movement, Nike`. The title shows in the notifications followers get when you go live. **[Whatnot]**
   - **Description:** list the brands and item types in plain words (leggings, sports bras, flare jeans, knit sweaters). Buyers find shows by those search words. **[Whatnot]**
   - **Thumbnail:** your cover from `02-branding/whatnot-covers/`, with a real photo of you in real inventory, not AI images. **[Whatnot]**
   - **Preview video (optional):** 20–30 seconds, vertical 9:16, showing the best pieces. **[Whatnot]**
2. **List every piece with detail:** brand, size, condition, photo and category. **[Whatnot]**
   - Turn on **pre-bids** for your heroes. Shows with strong pre-bid activity see up to 7× more total sales and nearly 4× more orders. Orders with a pre-bid have about 45% higher order value. **[Whatnot]**
   - Buyers who are outbid before the show get a notification to raise their bid. **[Whatnot]**
3. **Set up 3+ Buy It Now listings,** reserved for the show. **[Whatnot]**
4. **Set up giveaways** and paste the Official Rules into Show Notes (template below). **[Whatnot]**
5. **Share the show link** on Instagram/TikTok so people can bookmark it and get notified. **[Whatnot]**
6. **Check shipping profiles** match each item's real packed weight. Wrong weights cause carrier adjustments. **[Whatnot]**
7. **Only schedule shows you can ship within 2 business days.** **[Whatnot]**

## Show day, before going live

- [ ] Practice in **Rehearsal Mode** (Seller Hub → menu → Settings → Rehearsal Mode) or a private show if you're still new. **[Whatnot]**
- [ ] Phone on a stand or tripod. A shaky feed hurts the show. **[Whatnot]**
- [ ] Two softbox lights (two-point lighting). Whatnot's production guide says it balances shadows and reduces glare better than a ring light. **[Whatnot]**
- [ ] Rack the pieces **in running order**, with each measurement card and flaw note clipped to the piece.
- [ ] Open the **pre-bid filter** and move pre-bid pieces up the run. Whatnot says to "prioritize items buyers have already raised their hands for." **[Whatnot]**
- [ ] Post-its and a pen for winner names. **[Whatnot, Go Live Guide]**
- [ ] On a laptop, open **Live Analytics** (web only, "pop out" button). It shows viewers, orders, followers and average watch time in real time. **[Whatnot]**
- [ ] Show Notes posted (giveaway rules, shipping, condition line).

## Run of show (60 minutes)

Times are a template **[Test]**. The contents of each step are **[Whatnot]**.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0:00–1:30 | **Opening script** (below). Get the first piece on camera by 1:30. Whatnot: "People decide fast whether to stay or scroll. Start like the room is already full." |
| 1:30–8:00 | Regular pieces. Say the shipping line once. |
| ~8:00 | **Hero 1.** Pin it before you talk about it: pre-bidders get a notification when you pin their item. **[Whatnot]** |
| 8–15 | Regular pieces. |
| ~15:00 | **Repeat block** (intro + follow + Buy It Now line). |
| ~16:00 | **Hero 2** |
| ~20:00 | **Giveaway 1** (follower-only). Keep auctioning while it runs; that's allowed. **[Whatnot]** |
| 20–30 | Regular pieces + **Hero 3** |
| ~30:00 | Repeat block. Check Live Analytics: are viewers rising or falling? |
| 30–45 | Regular pieces + **Hero 4** + **Hero 5**. When viewers peak, run your best hero and **Boost** it. A Boost is up to 15 minutes of extra visibility, "best for when you have an important moment." **[Whatnot]** |
| ~45:00 | **Giveaway 2** + repeat block |
| 45–57 | Regular pieces + **Hero 6** |
| 57–60 | **Closing script**: thank-yous, bookmark the next show, then **raid** a friend's show. Raiding ends your stream and sends your viewers to them. **[Whatnot]** |

**Also do the repeat block whenever:** a raid comes in, a Community Boost
starts, or the viewer count jumps. Those bring new viewers who don't know
you yet. Whatnot ranks your next shows higher when new viewers stay, bid
and buy. **[Whatnot]**

## Exact wording

Change the words in [brackets]. Keep lines short. Only say things that are
true for that show.

### Opening (about 60–90 seconds)

Whatnot's top-seller formula is: quick intro → your story → shout out
repeat buyers → say thank you → ask a question. Repeat it through the show.
**[Whatnot]**

> "Hey, welcome in! I'm Jade, this is **Kenny Shop**, and tonight is
> **Premium [Activewear / Contemporary]**, everything starts at **one dollar**.
> I hand-pick every piece myself: [Lululemon, Free People Movement, Nike /
> Free People, Polo Ralph Lauren, premium denim], and I check every piece for
> flaws before it goes on camera. [@name], welcome back, thank you for
> shopping with me again! Thank you all for being here. What size are you
> hunting tonight? Drop it in the chat and I'll pull it for you. First piece
> coming right now."

(Only say "I check every piece" if you do. Buyers can ask you to run their size, so pull requested sizes early. **[Whatnot]**)

### Regular piece (about 50 words, about 20 seconds)

Whatnot **requires** you to say the condition out loud (or put it in the
listing), say every major flaw, and show the item from every side. The size
you give must be the tag size. **[Whatnot]**

The formula: **brand + item → tag size → condition → flaws (or "no flaws") →
turn it around → 2 measurements → one detail → start.**

**Activewear example:**
> "Lululemon Align high-rise pant, tag size 6, 25-inch inseam. Pre-owned,
> **excellent**: no pilling, no stains, no holes. *[front, back, inner
> thighs up close, waistband tag]* Waist is 13 inches flat. Color is black.
> One-dollar start, ten seconds. Here we go!"

**Contemporary example:**
> "Polo Ralph Lauren cable-knit sweater, tag size medium, 100% cotton.
> Pre-owned, **good**: light pilling under the arms, here it is up close.
> *[front, back, flaw close-up, tag]* 21 inches pit to pit, 25 long.
> One-dollar start, ten seconds. Go!"

**Denim example:**
> "[Brand] flare jeans, tag size 27. Pre-owned, **excellent**, no fading or
> fraying. *[front, back, hems, tag]* Waist 14 flat, rise 10, inseam 31.
> One-dollar start, ten seconds. Go!"

### Hero piece (45–60 seconds)

Showing the piece on camera is the step that lowers buyers' uncertainty.
Chatting with you and seeing other viewers' comments raise purchase intent
directly. **[Study]** For luxury, Whatnot tells sellers to "use close-ups,
natural lighting, and model [items] live." **[Whatnot]**

Before it (while the piece before it is running):
> "Next up is one of the best pieces of the night. Stay right here."

Then:
> "Okay, this one! [Brand] [item], tag size [X]. [New with tags / Pre-owned
> excellent]. Let me put it on so you can see the fit. *[try on or hold it
> against you, turn around, show the tag and fabric up close]* [One true
> detail: the style name, the fabric, NWT, the color name.] [Measurements.]
> One-dollar start, fifteen seconds. Let's go!"

### Winner (every sale, 2–3 seconds)
> "Congrats [@name]! Thank you!"

When a Jumbotron or special reaction pops up, call it out: "[@name] with
the Jumbotron, I see you!" Whatnot says to acknowledge these. More than 1 in
5 live orders now includes one. **[Whatnot]**

### Repeat block (about 30 seconds, every 10–15 min and after any raid or viewer jump)
> "If you just joined, welcome! I'm Jade, this is Kenny Shop, Premium
> [Activewear], one-dollar starts all night. [@repeat buyer], thank you!
> If you're new, tap **follow**. Follower giveaway at [time]. Can't stay?
> Tap the shop: the **Buy It Now** tab has [3] pieces you can grab right now."

Why: nearly half of buyers open the live shop as soon as they join, and
buyers short on time often buy right away if they find what they want.
**[Whatnot]**

### Shipping line (once early, once in the middle)
> "Everything you win tonight ships together in one box, and once your box
> is between 1 and 5 pounds, shipping stays at $7.75 no matter how many
> pieces you add."

Facts behind it **[Whatnot]**: Whatnot bundles one buyer's show purchases
automatically. USPS Ground Advantage on Whatnot is a flat $7.75 from 1 to 5
lb. Under 1 lb is priced by weight (for example $4.85 up to 8 oz). Alaska,
Hawaii and territories pay standard rates. Show buys and shop buys don't
bundle together. If you turn on a shipping cap in Seller Hub, say "Shipping
maxes out at $[cap] tonight" instead. Capped shows may get a "Reduced Shipping"
label that buyers can filter by.

### Giveaway line (every time you mention a giveaway)
Whatnot requires the words **"NO PURCHASE NECESSARY"** every time you
promote a giveaway. You must also say what the prize is before it runs.
**[Whatnot]**
> "Giveaway is live. Tap **Enter** at the top of your screen. **No purchase
> necessary.** The prize is [brand + item + size]. You have to be in the show
> when it draws, in five minutes. Full rules are in the show notes."

Ahead of time (allowed **[Whatnot]**):
> "Follower giveaway at [8:20] / when we hit [30] viewers."

### Closing (last 2–3 minutes)
> "That's the show! Thank you so much, [@top buyers], [@new buyers]. Every
> order ships within two business days. Tap **bookmark** on my next show:
> [day] is **Premium [Contemporary]** night, and you'll get a notification the
> second I go live. Now we're raiding my friend [@seller]. Go say hi!"

Whatnot's top sellers "prompt buyers to bookmark your show." **[Whatnot]**

## Never say or do these (Whatnot rules)

- "Buy something to get entered" or "spend $X to qualify." Requiring a purchase to win is prohibited. **[Whatnot]**
- "Follow my Instagram/TikTok to win." You can't add entry rules beyond Whatnot's built-in options (followers-only, domestic-only, buyers-plus-free-web-entry). **[Whatnot]**
- Refusing, downgrading or changing a prize based on who won. **[Whatnot]**
- "The winner gets it next show." Giveaways must finish in the same show. **[Whatnot]**
- Spin wheels or any randomizer outside Whatnot's giveaway tool. **[Whatnot]**
- Friends or family bidding to raise prices (shill bidding). This can get you banned. **[Whatnot]**
- Taking payment or sales off Whatnot. **[Whatnot]**
- Hiding a major flaw. Whatnot's list of major flaws: **rips, stains, discoloration, pilling, misshapen items, missing/broken closures, odors.** These must always be disclosed. Penalties include being billed back or banned. **[Whatnot]**

## Condition words to use (Whatnot's apparel categories)

| Say | Means | Must you say flaws? |
|---|---|---|
| New with tags | Unworn, tags on, no defects | Yes, any at all |
| New with defects | Unworn, minor imperfections, tags may be missing | Yes, every minor flaw |
| Pre-owned, excellent | No noticeable flaws | Major flaws only |
| Pre-owned, good | Minor flaws or wear | Major flaws only |
| Pre-owned, fair | Obvious flaws or wear | **Yes, in the livestream** |
| Pre-owned, damaged | Clear damage | **Yes, in the livestream** |

Source: Whatnot Help Center, "Apparel on Whatnot: Best Practices". Fit
issues ("runs big") don't count as misrepresentation. The tag size does.

## Premium Activewear notes

- **Flaw check before the show** (Whatnot's major-flaw list, applied to activewear): pilling (inner thighs, seat, underarms), a stretched or misshapen waistband, faded or discolored spots, broken zippers, holes, odor.
- **Measurements to say:** bottoms, waist flat + inseam. Tops and bras, pit to pit + length. Take them from the measurement card.
- **Say the brand and style name** when you know it: Align, Define, Wunder Train, Scuba. Whatnot tells sellers to "call out brands clearly during your shows". In its Beauty data, brand-specific searches convert better and branded shows sell about 43% more per show. **[Whatnot]**
- **Swimwear** has its own subcategory (Women's Swimwear, added April 2026). List swim pieces there. **[Whatnot]**
- **Hero ideas:** NWT sets, complete matching sets, Lululemon Define jackets, anything with pre-bids.

## Premium Contemporary notes

- **Flaw check before the show:** pilling on knits, underarm discoloration, missing buttons, broken zippers, denim hem fraying, odor.
- **Measurements to say:** tops/knits, pit to pit + length. Denim, waist flat + rise + inseam (+ leg opening for flares). Dresses, pit to pit + length.
- **Say the fabric** from the care tag (100% cotton, linen, silk, cashmere). It's a quick detail that answers a common question. **[Test]**
- **Trend words, if they truly apply,** from Whatnot's 2026 trend report **[Whatnot]**: *boho* (+920%), *western* (+1,065%), *fringe* (+515%), *embroidery* (+415%), *flowy* (+550%). In Whatnot's Dallas data, **Free People** and Target were the most-resold vintage/Y2K brands, and denim grew 270%, led by **barrel-leg, flare and straight** cuts.
- **Hero ideas:** Free People embroidered or boho pieces, Polo cable knits, premium denim in flare/barrel/straight cuts, anything with pre-bids.

## After the show

1. **Ship within 2 business days** (weekends and holidays don't count), and scan each package with the **Whatnot in-app scanner** at drop-off. A drop-off scan cuts shipping support tickets by up to 60%. **[Whatnot]**
2. Check **cancellation requests**. Buyers have 24 hours to ask, and you can approve or deny. **[Whatnot]**
3. Unsold pieces: run them next show, or move them to Buy It Now.
4. **Clip** your best moment (the last 60 seconds, from the app) and post it to Instagram/TikTok with the next show link. **[Whatnot]**
5. Fill in the log below from **Seller Analytics → Shows tab** (web). It keeps 30 days only, so log each show the same week. **[Whatnot]**

## What isn't proven (be skeptical of anyone who says it is)

- **Exact item count, talk time, hero spacing, how often to repeat the intro, best day/time to go live.** Whatnot doesn't publish these for fashion. Everything above on these is **[Math]** or **[Test]**. Your show log will answer them for your shop within 4–6 shows.
- **"30–50 items per show"** and **"30–60 second auctions drive higher bids"** come from third-party blogs with no data. Whatnot's own data says the opposite on timers: 5–10 second timers make the most sales per hour, and womenswear sellers' average timer is under 10 seconds. I didn't use either claim.
- **Promotion returns:** Whatnot says Promote tools return about 2.6× what you spend, *on average across sellers*. Your return may differ. Check the Marketing dashboard after each paid Boost.
