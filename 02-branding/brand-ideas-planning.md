# On-Brand Ideas & Planning

## Brand basics
- Shop names: KennyShop / Kurated by Kenny
- Vibe / aesthetic: curated, premium brands at $1 starts. Two show lines:
  - Premium Contemporary: quiet luxury (navy + white + butter yellow, serif headline)
  - Premium Activewear: bold, sporty (cobalt + white + neon lime, heavy block font)
- Brand colors: deep blue + white + one bright accent on both, so the two shows look related.
  Contemporary: navy `#1F2E4A` + butter yellow `#F6D776`.
  Activewear: cobalt `#1F3FD1` + neon lime `#D4FF3A`.
- Logo: `KURATED BY KENNY`, spaced-out caps in the accent color, top of every cover
- Who I'm selling to: Whatnot buyers hunting Aritzia / Free People / Reformation and
  Lululemon / Alo / Vuori deals
