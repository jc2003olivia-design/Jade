# Live-Show Branding Research (Whatnot, women's resale)

Covers: Premium Activewear and Modern Contemporary. Researched Sept 2026.

## How covers get seen
- **Phone size is small.** In the feed a cover shows about 150–200px wide, so anything you can't read at that size is just decoration. Only 3 things need to read small: **$1 STARTS**, the **category**, and **your face**.
- **Say the category.** People should know what they're clicking into. Brand logos do this fastest for resale fashion, since shoppers search and follow brands (Whatnot has brand pages like "lululemon").
- **Bold beats pastel.** Saturated colors stop the scroll, and pastels blend in. Dark on light (or light on dark) reads best small.

## Branding for clothing shows
- **Show your face.** Clothing buyers want to connect with the person styling, and "faceless" clothing shows do worse. A real, natural photo beats a staged or AI-looking one.
- **Be the same every time.** Use the same template, the same 2–3 colors, and your logo/shop name in the same spot every show, so regulars spot you without reading.
- **Cover and title should match.** Top activewear shows are titled like "$1 Starts, Activewear, Lululemon": price, then category, then top brands.
- **Don't oversell.** If the cover shows brands that aren't in the show, people click and leave fast, and that hurts your ranking.

## Show format & growth
- **$1 starts + sudden death** creates FOMO and fast bidding, and it's a popular format for clothing. Watch your margins on heavy or low-value pieces, because fees eat $1 sales.
- **Pick one weekly time slot and stick with it.** Showing up consistently matters more than the exact time.
- **Promote the day before** on IG/TikTok, using the cover plus 2–3 preview pieces.
- **The first minutes matter.** Whatnot pushes shows that pass viewer milestones (10/25/50/100).

## How the Kurated by Kenny covers apply this
- A real photo of you fills the cover, with 6 brand logos on the left (no more).
- **$1 STARTS** is the biggest thing, top-right, in hot pink. **10 SEC SUDDEN DEATH** is solid hot pink under it.
- The **category** is the second-biggest text. The shop name and live time are small.
- Brand kit: hot pink `#ff2d6f`, white, black, Fredoka font.
