# Thumbnail Maker

Whatnot covers live in `whatnot-covers/`. The research behind them is in `whatnot-covers/RESEARCH.md`.

## Style
- Background: a real, natural photo of me filling the whole cover (daylight, clean background, face clear)
- Lighting: daylight or golden hour, no filters or beauty mode
- Fonts / colors: Fredoka (rounded bold); hot pink `#ff2d6f`, white, black
- Logo placement: brand logos as white sticker tiles down the left (6 max). $1 STARTS top-right. Category, KURATEDBYKENNY and live time bottom-right.
